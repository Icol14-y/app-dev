# The Twilight Saga (2008-2012)

## Overview

**The Twilight Saga** is a series of five romantic fantasy films based on the novels by **Stephanie Meyer**, 
produced by *Summit Entertainment*. It follows **Bella Swan**, a teenager girl who moves to Forks, Washington,
and falls in love with **Edward Collin**, a vampire.

---

## The Five Films

| 1 | Twilight                                  | 2008 | Catherine Hardwicke      |
| 2 | The Twilight Saga: New Moon               | 2009 | Chris Weitz              |
| 3 | The Twilight Saga: Eclipse                | 2010 | David Slade              |
| 4 | The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn-Part 1   | 2011 | Bill Condon              |
| 5 | The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn-Part 2   | 2012 | Bill Condon              |

## Main Characters

1. **Bella Swan**: the human girl at the center of the story (Kristen Stewert)
2. **Edward Cullen**: the vampire Bella Swan falls in love with (Robert Pattinson)
3. **Jacob Black**: Bella Swan loyal friend, a warewolf (Taylor Lautner)
4. **The Cullens**: Edward's vampire family
5. **The Volturi**: the powerful vampire coven that enforces the rules
